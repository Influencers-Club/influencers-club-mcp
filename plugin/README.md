# Influencers Club for Claude

Influencers Club connects your AI assistant to creator data across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Twitch, and OnlyFans. Ask in plain language, "find fitness creators on Instagram with 100k+ followers and 3%+ engagement", and get back a ranked list you can act on.

## What you get

- Creator discovery with semantic search and filters for followers, engagement, location, and language
- Profile enrichment at different depths, from a quick profile to full analytics with contact details, audience demographics, and past brand deals
- Recent posts with engagement metrics, audience-overlap comparison between creators, and lookalike search for creators similar to one that already performs well
- Team exclusion lists that keep creators you have already contacted out of your searches

Built for influencer marketers, agencies, talent managers, and founders doing campaign research, prospecting, and creator vetting.

## How it works

This plugin contains no code that runs on your machine. It connects Claude to the hosted Influencers Club MCP server at `https://mcp-dashboard.influencers.club/mcp`. The first time you use it, you sign in with your Influencers Club account; no API key is needed. Results come from the Influencers Club API and are billed against your account credits, exactly as if you ran them in the dashboard.
